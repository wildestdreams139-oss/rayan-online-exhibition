SAME KID.
DIFFERENT WORLD.

Rayan moves through eight public galleries, an archive room, an after-hours threshold and two hidden horror rooms. Each room has its own pace and visual logic.

GUEST —VISITOR № ——
Take the route slowly. Open works for a closer view, switch languages at any time, and use MAP to jump between rooms. Your museum passport records each room you enter.

GALLERY 01 · QUIET DAYS / 静日
QUIET DAYS

Small gestures, close light.

The main exhibition opens with ordinary pauses: a window, a stair, a companion nearby. Rayan is observed between moments rather than staged for spectacle.

CURATOR NOTE · The main exhibition opens with ordinary pauses: a window, a stair, a companion nearby. Rayan is observed between moments rather than staged for spectacle.

GALLERY 02 · CAFÉ & STUDIO / 咖啡与工作室
CAFÉ & STUDIO

Places become portraits.

The café and studio shift attention from pose to environment. Tables, paper, windows and worn surfaces show how the character occupies a day.

CURATOR NOTE · The café and studio shift attention from pose to environment. Tables, paper, windows and worn surfaces show how the character occupies a day.

GALLERY 03 · TWILIGHT & REST / 黄昏与休憩
TWILIGHT & REST

Movement, then stillness.

A walk at dusk and a pause on a bench form a diptych about movement and rest. The background stays quiet so body language carries the mood.

CURATOR NOTE · A walk at dusk and a pause on a bench form a diptych about movement and rest. The background stays quiet so body language carries the mood.
ROOM 04 →
GALLERY 04 · IDENTITY & MOOD / 人物与情绪
IDENTITY & MOOD

The character becomes a sign.

Attitude, headphones, frog iconography and hand-painted marks are reduced into a recognizable exhibition language.

CURATOR NOTE · Attitude, headphones, frog iconography and hand-painted marks are reduced into a recognizable exhibition language.
ROOM 05 →
GALLERY 05 · INK & PRINT / 水墨与版画
INK & PRINT

Across East Asian picture traditions.

Ink-wash space and woodblock rhythm ask how much the world can change while the character remains unmistakably Rayan.

CURATOR NOTE · Ink-wash space and woodblock rhythm ask how much the world can change while the character remains unmistakably Rayan.
ROOM 06 →
GALLERY 06 · MURAL & OIL / 壁画与油画
MURAL & OIL

Surface, pigment, portrait.

Mineral-pigment mural language meets the gravity of an old-master portrait. Familiar clothes stay, while the pictorial rules change completely.

CURATOR NOTE · Mineral-pigment mural language meets the gravity of an old-master portrait. Familiar clothes stay, while the pictorial rules change completely.
ROOM 07 →
GALLERY 07 · LIGHT & NIGHT / 光与夜色
LIGHT & NIGHT

Daylight dissolves; night reflects.

The garden is built from broken daylight; the river scene gathers that light back into reflection. Together they form the most atmospheric room in the historical route.

CURATOR NOTE · The garden is built from broken daylight; the river scene gathers that light back into reflection. Together they form the most atmospheric room in the historical route.
ROOM 08 →
GALLERY 08 · ORNAMENT & FORM / 装饰与形式
ORNAMENT & FORM

From floral line to geometry.

The final art-history room moves toward design: floral framing becomes graphic structure, then gives way to geometry and controlled color fields.

CURATOR NOTE · The final art-history room moves toward design: floral framing becomes graphic structure, then gives way to geometry and controlled color fields.
